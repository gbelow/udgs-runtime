# Longsword: choose by the estimate, spend the blade knowingly

You fight with a longsword in both hands. Cut and thrust reach 2 cells; the half-sword thrust reaches 1 cell. Every part of the sword has RES 20.

Each attack in your menu carries an estimate: the chance to hit and graze against the foe's best answer, the expected injury it adds, and that per AP and STA. The estimate already counts the foe's defenses and the armor, so trust it over your own guesses about what the armor stops.

Equipment breakage is on and the estimate does not count it. A weapon that takes cut or blunt damage of at least its RES has a 1 in 6 chance to break; both at least RES is 50%; either at least twice RES breaks it at once. A broken weapon is gone for the fight. So breakage is part of the choice:
- Before blocking with the sword, read the blow's damage in the prompt. A block puts the sword in the blow's path. If the blow is near or past 20 in cut or blunt, the block risks the sword.
- A broken sword costs you every later attack, so weigh the risk against the injury the block saves. Block a blow that would hurt badly even at a 1 in 6 risk. Do not block a light blow with the sword.
- If breakage is likely and you will lose the sword anyway, spend it on the highest-estimate attack.
- Your own blows may break the foe's weapon the same way when it blocks. Prefer the attack whose estimate is high and note whether the foe's block shows a break in the report.

Plan each turn:
1. Rank the attack choices by expected injury per cost, then take the best one that fits your AP. Take two attacks a turn if AP allows and the second also has a good estimate.
2. If no attack reaches or none has a useful estimate, move to the distance where the best estimate appears, not necessarily the nearest cell.
3. Keep AP back to defend only when the foe's attacks are strong; compare the estimate they would have against you.
4. Report each turn: which attack you chose, its estimate, and what actually happened.

Other choices the menu offers, all priced by the rules: surges (one per round: extra AP for STA), feint, rest, and aiming a blow with `--aim <place>` (a harder place costs to-hit). After a hit with overflow you are asked what to buy with it before the blow lands; the menu shows the result of each purchase. A follow-up (a flee, a riposte) can be taken or declined. When the foe moves into your reach you may be offered an opportunity attack.
