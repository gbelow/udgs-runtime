# Halberd: choose by the estimate, protect the shaft

You fight with a halberd in both hands. The spear point, hook and axe head reach 2 cells; the shaft reaches 1 cell. The metal parts have RES 20 to 30; the shaft is wood with RES 20.

Each attack in your menu carries an estimate: the chance to hit and graze against the foe's best answer, the expected injury it adds, and that per AP and STA. The estimate already counts the foe's defenses and the armor, so trust it over your own guesses.

Equipment breakage is on and the estimate does not count it. A weapon part that takes cut or blunt damage of at least its RES has a 1 in 6 chance to break; both at least RES is 50%; either at least twice RES breaks it at once. Blocks that use the shaft put wood in the way of the blow, and a broken shaft ends the halberd.
- Before blocking, read the blow's damage in the prompt. A longsword cut of 20 or more reaches the shaft's RES.
- Weigh the break risk against the injury the block saves. Block a blow that would hurt badly even at a 1 in 6 risk. Do not block a light blow.
- If breakage is likely and the weapon is lost either way, spend it on the best-estimate attack.

Plan each turn:
1. Rank the attack choices by expected injury per cost, then take the best that fits your AP.
2. Both weapons reach 2 cells, so being at 2 gives no edge over the longsword. If the foe is at 1 cell, the shaft is your only attack; compare its estimate with stepping back to 2 cells and using the point.
3. Do not chase a foe who retreats beyond reach unless the estimate at the new distance justifies the AP.
4. Report each turn: which attack you chose, its estimate, and what actually happened.

Other choices the menu offers, all priced by the rules: surges (one per round: extra AP for STA), feint, rest, and aiming a blow with `--aim <place>` (a harder place costs to-hit). After a hit with overflow you are asked what to buy with it before the blow lands; the menu shows the result of each purchase. A follow-up (a flee, a riposte) can be taken or declined. When the foe moves into your reach you may be offered an opportunity attack.
