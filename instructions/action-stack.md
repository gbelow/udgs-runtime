# Action stack refactor

Progress tracker for reorganizing combat actions around one linear pipeline and an
explicit resolution stack. Update the stage checklist and the log as work lands.

## The model

Every action runs the same pipeline:

`define → react → roll → post → [actions its reactions opened] → effect → [follow-ups]`

- **define**: the actor declares, aims and commits. The commit is final.
- **react**: everyone the committed action triggers something in may answer, one answer
  per character. Reactions always come before the roll, spells included.
- **roll**: the die (or the payment, for an action with none) and every price, in one step.
- **post**: the choices made once the result is known — HOP, a disarm pick, a push's
  direction, spell improvements. Reversible until confirmed.
- **effect**: the action lands and generates new actions.

Two kinds of answer:

- A **reaction** answers the declaration. It is part of its parent's `react` step and
  never has reactions of its own. When it opens an action (an opportunity attack opens a
  strike), that action is played out before the parent's effect (combat.tex
  "Opportunity Attack": "The attack occurs before the effect of the triggering action").
- A **follow-up** answers the result, and is generated by the parent's effect: an
  opportunity attack reaching a mover's stretch, a riposte after a miss, the trample of a
  braced attack. Some are declared in advance and fired by the result, some are offered
  after it and may be declined.

Anything that can be reacted to is an action of its own, so reactions to reactions are
reactions to the action a reaction opened. Resolution is recursive through the stack.

Rulings the stack must respect:

- An interruption breaks the interrupted action: no effect beyond where it stopped, and no
  follow-ups. Running and jumping carry on.
- Every declared reaction resolves, in order. A move's opportunity attacks on stretches it
  never reaches do not fire.
- An action writes only its own record, and only up to its own effect. Nothing is written
  onto another action after that action's commit.
- Opportunity attacks never trigger other opportunity attacks. What one opens (a strike,
  a maneuver, a push) is still answered by its target, and draws no opportunity attack.
- A counterattack (abilities.tex "Counterattack") is a reaction whose opened attack goes
  before the parent's effect if it rolled higher, and is a follow-up if it rolled lower.
  On a tie both land and neither interrupts the other. It answers strikes only.
- A riposte follows the `.tex`: after an active defense against a melee attack that
  misses or grazes, if in range; 1 AP less only with an object other than the one that
  defended, and after an evade (which uses no object) always 1 AP less.
- The braced trample stays a fact on the braced strike's own record: it has no die and no
  choice, so an action of its own would have nothing to decide.

## Target shape

- `ActionBase.step`: `define | react | post | done`, replacing `status`. The roll and the
  effect are transitions, not places an action waits, so they have no value.
- `CombatState.stack`: action ids, the top being the action the table is waiting on.
  `getOpenAction` reads the top.
- One `advance(state)` in `commands/sequence.ts` owns sequencing: pushes the actions the
  reactions open, runs the effect, pushes the follow-ups, resumes the frame below on pop.
- One `isInterrupted(state, id)` rule replacing `isCancelled`, `getPushStop` and the
  interruption part of `getMoveOverride`.
- Each reaction kind in `ACTIONS` says where its opened action goes:
  `opens: 'before' | 'after' | 'byRoll'`. Dropped at stage 7: every reaction that opens
  something needs a rule of its own to say when (a reflex by its degree, a counterattack
  by comparing two results), so the field would only name which rule to call.

## Decisions

- **Flanker order** — decided: *before* the strike lands. "Can be voided if the target
  gets out of range" is checked when each flanker's attack comes to be fought.
- **Chained opportunity attacks** — decided: none. This also removes the third-party
  attacks a push made *as* an opportunity attack used to draw.
- **Push window** — decided: a generated action (`displace`) carries the displacement.
- **Saved fights** — not needed yet. Nothing ingests a `CombatState` today (the store only
  parses `{}`). When a saved fight is first loaded, rebuild the stack from the log:
  unresolved roots in log order, those opened by a reaction on top.
